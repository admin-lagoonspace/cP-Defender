Every reading

The CDN manifest is read twice on purpose: once normally, once with a cache-busting parameter. That pair is the whole diagnosis. If the two disagree, a proxy cache is holding an old copy over a correct origin. If they agree but trail the published version, the origin itself is behind and the sync has not run.
